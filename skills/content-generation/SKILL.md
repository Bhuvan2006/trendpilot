# Content generation

Before writing, call `get_learnings` and follow any human feedback that applies, then call `search_brand_knowledge` for the topic's pillar.

## LinkedIn
A hook line, then a short body that teaches one idea, then a question or call to action. 120 to 220 words. Name the source of any outside finding.

## Instagram
A punchy first line, two or three short lines, a call to action, and three to five hashtags. Under 120 words.

## Rules
- Mention a Dots-In product only as the company describes it, phrased as "Dots-In describes..." or similar.
- Use only numbers that appear in the source item or brand knowledge.
- Never write "world's only", "world's first", "guaranteed", "clinically proven", or make health outcome promises.
- Say clearly when something is an idea and not an established finding.
