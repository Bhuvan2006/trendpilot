# Dots-In brand knowledge

Source for every entry: the public website https://www.dotsin.ai/, read on 8 November 2026. These are the company's own statements, not independently verified, and have not been reviewed by Dots-In. Each `##` heading is a content pillar and each bullet is one searchable entry.

## Behavioural AI
- Dots-In describes itself as a behavioural intelligence app, available on iOS and Android, with a Chrome extension.
- The company says its feed ranks reels using a Relevancy Score and a Behavioral State Vector to predict what matters now.
- Write about modelling behaviour as a topic. Do not state that Dots-In's model outperforms any other model.

## Living Twin
- The company describes the Living Twin as a digital twin that grows and syncs with the user every 30 minutes.
- The company says the Living Twin helps users understand their health, habits and behavioural patterns, and find relevant people, content and opportunities.

## Zero-Prompt AI
- The company describes a Zero Prompt Agent Harness in which agents perform tasks with less back-and-forth than traditional AI.
- Meetings feature, as described by the company: summaries, speakers, causation and tasks are captured automatically across conversations.

## Causal intelligence
- The company shows a CBG Graph that it says maps causal links between experiences, behaviours, choices and outcomes.
- The company describes a Personalized Action and Goal Tracker that sets goals and steps toward a future self, and Next Best Actions.

## Privacy-first AI
- The company says its Chrome extension keeps personalisation privacy-first.
- Do not make specific claims about how data is stored, processed or protected, because the public page does not explain this.

## Tone
- Bold, aspirational and direct. The company's slogans include "Live Limitless" and "Upgrade Your Reality". Keep posts technically honest and free of unverifiable superlatives.
